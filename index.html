FAQ

Common questions

Can I try the app before paying?

Yes. The free version includes 2 questions in every sector, a free AI review and re-score, a free coach message and a free interview pack preview, so you can see how it works before you pay anything.

Can it prepare me for a specific job advert?

Yes — paste the full job description into the app and it generates a tailored interview pack for that exact role: 12 likely questions, the competencies the panel will probe hardest (with evidence quoted from the advert), and tactical preparation insights.

Is the app really free to download?

Yes. Interview Coach UK is free to download on iOS and Android, and the free version includes 2 questions in every sector, a free AI review and re-score, a free coach message and a free interview pack preview. To unlock everything, choose the Interview Pass (£9.99, one payment for 30 days, does not renew) or Weekly (£2.99 a week, renews until cancelled).

What is the STAR method?

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. It's the most widely used framework for answering competency-based interview questions in the UK. The app teaches you the method and gives you feedback on how well you're applying it.

Which sectors are covered?

NHS, Civil Service, Technology, Finance, Education, Retail, Engineering, Marketing, and Universal (suitable for any role).

How do I cancel?

The Interview Pass is a single payment for 30 days. It does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel. Weekly renews every week until you cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, and you keep access until the end of the week you have paid for.
